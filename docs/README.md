# Docs index

Every file under `docs/` is listed here once, in the order a reader should
go through them. This page is regenerated by hand; `cargo test` fails if a
file is added or removed here without a matching change on disk.

## Start here

- **README.md** — this index.
- **WORKFLOWS.md** — what a workflow is, how its actions and contracts are
  declared, and how a task moves through one.
- **ACTIONS.md** — the two kinds of step a workflow can take (directive and
  operation) and the verification boundary between them.
- **CLIENT.md** — the contract every client (`forge-tui`, `forge-web`, the
  portal) is held to: talk to the `forge` CLI only, never the database or
  the kernel directly.

## Design

- **PROJECTS.md** — the two layers above a task, projects and initiatives,
  and what each is for.
- **JOBS.md** — workflows that run to produce an effect (a webhook, a
  scheduled rule) instead of landing a change, and what stays the same.
- **DEPLOY.md** — what a deploy target is, how a deploy runs, and what is
  deliberately not built.
- **INTAKE.md** — how a person who does not think in workflows tells Forge
  what to build, and how Forge confirms what they mean before building it.
- **PORTAL.md** — the customer-facing view of a project, as opposed to the
  operator's factory view.
- **PLUGINS.md** — integrations that live outside the kernel and talk to
  Forge the way any client does.
- **ECONOMIST.md** — how Forge rebalances spend across providers and
  workflows from its own measured record.
- **SUPERVISOR.md** — the read-only agent that answers a blocked task's
  question or a demoted review from the repository's own record.
- **GTM.md** — a working document on whether and how to sell what Forge
  produces to small businesses.

## Operations

- **OPS.md** — how to operate the store the operator's own Forge home runs
  against, and what to do when the unattended job that guards it fails.
- **CHECKS.md** — what Forge asks of itself on a schedule with no operator
  watching: the daily doctor, the weekly drift and engineering checks, and
  the weekly economist rebalance.
- **NATE.md** — decisions and actions only the operator can take; everything
  else keeps moving on its own.
- **ROADMAP.md** — what "1.0" means for Forge, what is done, and what is
  left, in the order it should land.

## Measurements

- **SYSTEM.md** — a generated map of the kernel's own modules and how they
  call each other.

## History

Everything below this line describes what was true when it was written; none
of it is kept up to date, and it should be read as a record, not as current
documentation.

- **REVIEW.md** — the first architectural review, six days into Forge 2, and
  the refactor plan that came out of it.
- **REVIEW-2.md** — the second architectural review, ten days in, after the
  first review's plan had landed.
- **LATER.md** — ideas from Forge 1's backlog worth revisiting later, and
  which of them have already been ported.
- **CONTEXT.md** — a running status note on what a coding agent is told
  before it starts looking at a task.
- **research/** — four external reports on verification and merge queues,
  and a synthesis of what they mean for Forge.
