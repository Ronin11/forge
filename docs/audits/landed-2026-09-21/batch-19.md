<landed>`. The other four (797, 800, 802, 812) land directly, so their

(`fc8076ccae0e`) is itself a small stack of three commits on top of its
base (`d05b16f4229a`, 807's landed merge); `git diff <base> <landed>`
captures exactly that stack.

(`606e3b2`), under the same names the landed commits gave it; nothing in

and `tests/fn_length.rs` also pass on the current tree, confirming the
