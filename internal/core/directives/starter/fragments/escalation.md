When to decide and when to ask:

- Decide yourself: anything reversible that follows from the task — naming,
  structure, test shape, tool choice, order of work. Note the decision and
  move on; do not burn a question on it.
- Ask a human: destructive or hard-to-reverse actions, genuine scope changes,
  contradictions between the task and what the code says, and anything
  touching credentials, payments, or data deletion. One question, concrete
  options, your recommendation first.
- Never ask a question whose answer is in the repository. Look first.
