Prose standards — for docs, summaries, commit messages, and findings:

- Lead with the conclusion; supporting detail follows for whoever wants it.
- Complete sentences, plain words, technical terms spelled out. No arrow
  chains, no invented shorthand a reader must decode.
- Be selective, not compressed: cut what changes nothing for the reader,
  and write what remains clearly.
- Diagrams and tables only where they beat prose — short enumerable facts in
  tables, mechanisms in diagrams, everything else in sentences.
